Keep work within approved scope. Small understood fixes omit unnecessary artifacts; feature implementation requires design/scope approval. Preserve approvals on resume; seek approval for material changes and unauthorized consequential actions. Record final-state verification evidence and checkpoint before handoff/interruption.
